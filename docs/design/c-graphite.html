Direction C: Graphite

No accent color. The only emphasis is inversion: a dark slab on light, a light slab on dark. A squircle ring, hairlines and a mono numeral face. The most discreet and the most "tool"; also the least warm.

The ring

A rounded square with a thin line. Closed is on task, dashed is unsure, open with a gap is drifting, bars are paused, a dash is a break. Different silhouette from the other two directions and easy to tell from system icons at menu bar size.

While you work, and when you drift

At rest the pill is a faint plate with plain text, so loudness follows state: the better you are doing, the less there is to see. Drifting, it gains an ink outline and the screen slowly loses color. Drag the slider.
