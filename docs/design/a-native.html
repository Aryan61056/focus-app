Direction A: Native

The app borrows the system's accent and materials and lets the ring and the words carry the identity. It should look like it shipped with the OS. Safest for trust; least ownable.

The ring

One hairline circle in the menu bar. Solid is on task, dashed is unsure, open with a gap is drifting, bars are paused, a dot is a break. Shown at menu bar size and at 4x.

While you work, and when you drift

On task, the pill is a quiet capsule. Drifting, the screen slowly loses color and the pill swaps its time for an off-topic count. On Mac the drain is a gray wash over the screen, not true desaturation. Drag the slider.
