Integration by parts

Calculus practice sheet. Work through these examples and explain each substitution in your own words.

The rule

Integral of u dv = uv minus the integral of v du. Choose u so its derivative is simpler, then integrate dv.

Exercise 1: integral of x e^x dx

Choose u = x and dv = e^x dx. Then du = dx and v = e^x. The result is x e^x minus e^x plus a constant.

Exercise 2: integral of ln x dx

Choose u = ln x and dv = dx. Then du = dx/x and v = x. The result is x ln x minus x plus a constant.
