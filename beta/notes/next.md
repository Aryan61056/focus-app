# Notes for the next beta. One change per line, in plain words a tester understands. Lines starting with # are left out.
- Hushd updates itself: it checks once a day, downloads a newer beta quietly, and offers Restart to update between sessions, so you never download it again
- Your beta key goes in when you first open Hushd (one key works on two Macs), and Settings has a License page
- A new setup flow in Liquid Glass, and a new drag-to-Applications window
- The open notch is real Liquid Glass now, black where it meets the hardware notch
- Send feedback from your name at the bottom left, then post it on GitHub: Hushd saves the report and opens the form, you drag the file in
- The Beta page shows your days left, whether this Mac is ready to test, and seven things to try
- A wrong-call report can carry Hushd's last calls (on, unsure or off, and how it decided), never the window
- Hushd notices when it closed unexpectedly and offers to write a report
- One quick question a week about how Hushd is working for you
